Pace

Hop with ← ↑ ↓ → or W A S D, or tap the side of the frog you want to go. Space starts and pauses. Cars squash, water drowns, and logs carry you along. Fill every bay to move up a level, before the bar runs out. Want a different board? Pick one above, or build your own below.

Build your own board

Rows run top to bottom. The first is the bank with the bays you are aiming for and the last is where the frog starts, so the rows in between are yours. Road and river can go in any order, as many of each as you like.
