Move with ← → or A D, or just drag inside the court. Space launches the ball and pauses. The wall is worth more at the top: diamond rows pay 50, staff rows pay 10. Every wall you clear comes back as a different shape with a quicker ball, and bricks with a bright outline take two hits for double the points.

Bricks sometimes leave a capsule behind. It falls straight down and only counts if the paddle catches it: 3 splits the rally into three more balls, W widens the paddle and S narrows it until the wall is cleared, and the rare + is a spare life. You only lose a life when the last ball goes.
