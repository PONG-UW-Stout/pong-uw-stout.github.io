Left click opens a square, right click flags one. On a phone, press and hold to flag. Clicking a number that already has the right count of flags around it opens everything else touching it, which is how the game is played at any speed.

The first square you open is never a mine, and never a number either: the field is laid after that click with its whole neighbourhood kept clear, so every game starts with a flood rather than a guess. The counter is mines minus flags, so it goes negative if you over-flag. The clock stops at 999, same as it always did.

Keyboard: arrow keys move, Enter or Space opens, F flags. Boards are kept per difficulty, fastest first.
