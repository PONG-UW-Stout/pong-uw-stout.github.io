PONG has been running LAN parties at UW-Stout since before most of its current members were in school. This section is the club's attempt to write that down properly, before any more of it disappears.

It was reconstructed from org renewal paperwork, a decade of meeting minutes, old budget filings, signed waivers, poster working files, two forum databases, a homegrown LAN portal's backup, and a very long recording made one night in October 2026 by two execs going through everything they could remember.

Where something is uncertain, it says so. Where something is missing, it says that too.

## Help us fill the gaps

The archive has holes in it, and most of them are in living memory.

We know almost nothing about the treasurers between 2007 and 2011. The vice presidents for most of the 2000s are missing entirely. There is a nine year stretch from 2017 onwards where we have the names but almost none of the paperwork, because the conversation moved to Discord and nobody was archiving it.

If you were there, the exec board would like to hear from you.
