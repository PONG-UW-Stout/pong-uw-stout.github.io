Welcome to the website for the People's Organization of Network Gaming!


## Around the Site
### Home

On the home page you can see the upcoming LAN, an invite to our discord, and our latest posts!

### About

This is where you can go to get all the latest information on the people and mentors behind PONG, thanks to everyone here, PONG is able to continue to run for years to come. Everyone on there has their own links too, so you can reach any of us directly. Our sponsors are on that page as well, and we would not be able to run the LANs we do without them!

### Events

Our calendar! Here is where you can see whats upcoming for PONG! You can flip it between the month view and a list of what is coming up next, whichever one you like.

### LANs

A list of old and upcoming LANs! Each LAN has its own customized theme page with all the information you need for the LAN. Go have a look at [LAN-Prix](/lans/LAN-Prix/) and [LAN-tasy](/lans/LAN-tasy/) to see what we mean!

### Posts

You are reading one right now! This is where we put LAN recaps, club news, and write ups on the things we build.

## Games

We have <span class="number">5</span> games, and you can play every one of them right here in your browser! No download, no account, nothing to install.

Each game keeps your best <span class="number">5</span> runs. They save in your browser, so they are yours, and nobody can knock you off your own leaderboard!

- [PONG](/games/pong/) is the one we are named after. Play a friend, or play the computer.
- [Breakout](/games/breakout/) has you clearing the wall without dropping the ball
- [Frogger](/games/frogger/) is all about crossing the traffic and the river to fill every bay. You can build your own board and send it to a friend as a link!
- [LAN-Prix Dodger](/games/lan-prix-dodger/) puts you on the LAN-Prix straight, overtaking the whole field without collecting anyone.
- [Guess the Exec](/games/guess-the-exec/) is like Wordle, but with people. There is a new one every day!

## Pick a Theme

The sun and moon button up in the nav bar switches the site between light and dark, and it remembers what you picked!

Everything else happens on its own. The site dresses up for the day, so keep an eye out on Halloween, Valentine's, St. Patrick's and Christmas, and there are a few more hiding in there for you to find. It also flies the flag on days worth marking, like Trans Day of Visibility, Intersex Awareness Day and Trans Day of Remembrance.

All through June a palette button shows up next to the theme toggle. That one is yours to play with! Pick whichever flag you want and the site wears it for the rest of the month.

## Where Else to Find Us

[Discord](https://discord.gg/pong) is where most of it actually happens! It is the fastest way to reach any of us, and it is the first place a LAN gets announced, so that is the one to join.

You can also find us on [Instagram](https://www.instagram.com/ponguwstout/), [Twitch](https://www.twitch.tv/ponguwstout), [Steam](https://steamcommunity.com/groups/PONG) and [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/company/ponguwstout/). Our [Linktree](https://linktr.ee/uwstoutpong) has the lot in one place, and you can always email us at [pong@uwstout.edu](mailto:pong@uwstout.edu).

## Want to Help?

Show up! Come to a LAN, say hi in our discord, and talk to an exec if you want to do more. There is always something that needs doing, whether that is running an event, sitting on the door, or moving tables on setup day.

This website is open source too! It all lives on [GitHub](https://github.com/PONG-UW-STOUT), so if you find something broken, or you want to add a game of your own, send us a pull request.

See you at the next LAN!
