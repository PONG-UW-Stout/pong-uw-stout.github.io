In the cartoon *Gravity Falls*, a character called Mabel makes a drink called Mabel Juice. It is bright, it has things floating in it, and it contains at least one plastic dinosaur. PONG now makes it for real, at more or less every LAN.




## The first batch

The first attempt was regular Red Bull mixed with Sour Patch Kids, and it ended up going down the sewer.

## Iterating

The second batch is where it turned into a real thing. The recipe moved to combinations of Monster, lemon-lime soda and various fruit punches, then split into two parallel versions, since PONG is sponsored by Red Bull and it seemed only fair to have a Red Bull variant.

The show has two versions as well. The green one sits in the fridge with a troll figure in it. The red one lives in a blender with plastic dinosaurs. In one episode the green version has dice floating in it, matching the plot where the characters get trapped inside a board game.

The club is clearly trying to match the source material. One exec's stated goal is to bring the dinosaurs back, and a troll has been proposed for comic book accuracy.

## The current state of the art

It gets made at essentially every LAN now, themed to that LAN where possible, and assembled to look, in the words of one of the people who makes it, "as crack head energy as possible simply for the enjoyment of everyone around us". It has been an official fixture for about three years.

There is one review on record, from Ryan Hawkins:

> everytime i take a sip of mable juice i can feel my heart in pain

## Things no longer in Mabel Juice

The first LAN version contained Monster, Sprite, Hawaiian Punch, plastic dinosaurs and edible glitter. The edible glitter is gone. So are the dinosaurs, for unrelated reasons, which the current board considers a loss and would like to reverse.
