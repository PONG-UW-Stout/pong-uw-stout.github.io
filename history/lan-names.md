PONG has never once called a LAN party a LAN party.

The pun habit is the single most consistent thing in the club's history. It survives every board, every venue change, every gap in the paperwork. You can lose nine years of meeting minutes and still reconstruct the timeline from event names alone, because somebody always wrote them down somewhere.

## The catalogue

PONG's first recognized event was in **2002**, but nothing from it survives with a name on it. What follows is every named event we can document. Two machine sources carry most of it: the photo gallery folders from the old WordPress site, which run 2004 to 2013, and the sub-forum names from the phpBB forums, which run 2011 to 2015. After that the trail picks up again in the club's own LAN pages.

### The modern run, 2021 onward

Four a year now, roughly September, November, February and April, each one 48 hours. In earlier years the club ran as many as six in an academic year, each of them shorter. Full details on each are on the [LANs page](/lans/).

## The posters

The names drove the art, and the art was mostly made in Photoshop by whoever held the designer seat.

 Muffin designed for the club around 2010, worked in composites: game characters dropped onto a background, usually zombies. The Winter WonderLAN 2009 poster is a Left 4 Dead 2 piece with the tagline **"Zombies don't mind the cold."**

The March Fools LAN poster is a Trackmania screenshot with a car circled in orange and the caption **"This could be you..."** It carries three sponsor logos along the bottom: Cooler Master, the Mabel Tainter Center for the Arts, and BAWLS Guarana. The prize was a free copy of Trackmania.

OctÜberFest 2011 went with **"Play Video Games, Maggots!"**
